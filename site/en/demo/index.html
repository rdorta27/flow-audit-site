Executive summary

An order takes 69 hours. It should take less than one. Nobody works 60 of those hours: they are a gap between systems that no process owns.

Priority: add capacity_profile to the technical catalog (USD 18,750/month). Retry provisioning before the manual queue (USD 12,750/month). Compensate the 48-hour timer (USD 14,104/month). The three actions cover 80% of the cost.

Where the days go

work by a system manual wait gap with no event

The gap starts when the first attempt fails. It ends when someone retries by hand 60 hours later. In that interval there is no event: no declared wait and no scheduled retry.
