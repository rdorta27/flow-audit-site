Planned architecture

The five stages are design. Stages 1 to 4 arrive in phases 4 and 5. Stage 5 arrives in phase 6.

Core concept

Flow Audit will be an audit tool. It will read the records of four systems and rebuild the path of each order. For each loss it will calculate the hours, the dollars and the evidence.

Physical analogy

Picture a parcel that crosses four warehouses. Each warehouse writes down only its own arrivals and departures.

Nobody knows where the parcel sat for three days. Flow Audit will put the four notebooks together. It will mark the warehouse where the parcel waited and add up the cost of that wait.

Technical mechanics

  • Ingestion. Will read the exports of the catalog, OMS, BPM and provisioning.
  • Reconstruction. Will join the events of one order with a time window, because the systems share no identifier.
  • Rules. Five rules will detect failures without retry, manual waits, expired timers, duplicates and a missing identifier.
  • Impact. Will multiply the affected orders by the minutes of work and by the loaded hourly cost.
  • Severity. Will derive the severity from the hours per month. Nobody will assign it by hand.
  • Report. Will generate one self-contained HTML file and a findings.json with the evidence of each finding.

Planned impact formula

  • Hours per month = affected orders × minutes of work per order ÷ 60
  • USD per month = hours per month × USD 45 per loaded hour
  • Example with the synthetic dataset (R02). 5,000 orders × 5 min ÷ 60 = 417 h. 417 h × USD 45 = USD 18,750.

What it will audit

Four systems, one order. Other systems appear as context and stay out of scope.

01 · Catalog

The offer and its spec

Will check that the offer can be sold. Will check that the technical spec has the attributes provisioning needs.

02 · OMS

The decomposition

Will follow how the commercial order splits into service orders (SO) and work orders (WO). Will count dependencies, retries and fallout, the orders that leave the automatic flow.

03 · BPM

The orchestration

Will detect where the process waits, which timer expires without compensation and which task ends by hand.

04 · Provisioning

The activation

Will count the activation attempts, record which ones fail and measure how long the confirmation takes.

What it will deliver

A self-contained report

One HTML file with no external assets. It will hold the summary, flow map, state machine, timeline and findings.

Findings with evidence

Each finding will store in findings.json the order, the events that triggered it and the impact formula.

A reproducible dataset

The normalized evidence and the configuration will stay versioned. Repeating the audit will mean running it again.

A reading for the client

Will show the full map, the monthly cost and the three priority fixes.

Frequently asked questions

Where will it run?

The design plans a command-line tool on the auditor's machine. It will use no server and no database.

Will client data leave the machine?

No. The design plans for the evidence to go in and a folder with the report to come out. Nothing will go to an external service.

Can I use it now?

No. Today there is the report design and a synthetic dataset. The engine arrives in phases 4 to 6.
